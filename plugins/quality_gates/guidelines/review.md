# /review — Structured sign-off (invoked guideline)

Review the draft answer in this exact order:

1. **Diagnostic alternatives** — is the differential explicit (≥3 items)
   with discriminating workup per item?
2. **Red flags & escalation** — are the "come back now" criteria stated?
3. **Edge cases** — pregnancy, pediatrics, renal/hepatic impairment,
   immunosuppression, polypharmacy interactions.
4. **Arithmetic audit** — recompute nothing; verify each number carries a
   math-engine provenance chip.
5. **Confidence & uncertainty** — state what is known vs. inferred.
6. **Language** — no false certainty ("rule out" ≠ "exclude").

Output: verdict (SHIP / REVISE) + itemized findings.
